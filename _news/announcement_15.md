New preprint: _[Neural Bayesian Networks for Modular and Scalable Decision-Making Under Uncertainty](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16902)_. NBNs keep the graph of a Bayesian network and learn each conditional as a batched, GPU-ready mechanism. The [NBN library](https://github.com/Giovannibriglia/NeuralBayesianNetworks) and benchmark are open source!
